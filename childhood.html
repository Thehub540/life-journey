My Childhood

I was born and raised in Nairobi, a vibrant city that shaped much of my early experiences. From a young age, I was curious and eager to explore the world around me. My childhood was filled with adventures, learning, and the development of dreams that would later influence my life path.

Growing up, I had a keen interest in aeronautical engineering, fascinated by the idea of flight and the mechanics behind it. Although life took me in a different direction towards the tech industry, those early aspirations instilled in me a passion for innovation and problem-solving that continues to drive me today.
